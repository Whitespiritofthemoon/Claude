3. **Monetization keyword** (shopping intent):
   - "amazon finds", "amazon outfit", "shop the look", "affordable", "under $50"

### 2.2 Copy limits and structure
From Pinterest Business creative specs:
- Title: up to 100 chars (first ~40 chars most visible)
- Description: up to 500 chars
- Vertical format: 2:3 (1000x1500)

### 2.3 Hashtags in 2026
Operationally, Pinterest discovery is keyword- and relevance-driven. Use hashtags lightly (0–3 max, optional) only as support, not as the core SEO strategy.
